About Me

Welcome to my portfolio website! I'm Supreetha, a Senior Data Analyst with eight years of experience across NextGenSolutions, Infosys, and Accenture. I love working with data, whether it's building dashboards in Power BI and Tableau, writing SQL queries, or using Python to clean up messy datasets. I like to simplify things. If a report or a dashboard can't tell the story clearly, it's not done yet. I have an eye for the small details that make a big difference in how data gets understood and used.

Over the years I've also enjoyed mentoring junior analysts, leading requirements sessions with stakeholders, and helping teams stay aligned on what the data is really saying. I'm always curious and open to learning, and I genuinely believe that the best work happens when everyone on the team feels heard and supported.

Outside of work, I love travelling and exploring new places, reading, gardening, and trying out new recipes in the kitchen. Staying curious in all parts of life is something I carry into my work every day.

My work spans product, operations, marketing, revenue, finance, risk, healthcare, supply chain, and commerce analytics. My portfolio includes production-style projects in SaaS growth, FinTech credit risk, GL reconciliation, operations control towers, supply chain optimization, and CRM/ERP sales and customer analytics. Each project is built around a clear business question, reliable data models, and dashboards that help leaders decide what to do next.

Feel free to explore my projects and reach out if you'd like to connect on LinkedIn or check out my work on GitHub!
